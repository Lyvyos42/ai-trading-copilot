## 1. The Claim Under Test

> A 12-month (252-session) time-series momentum filter paired with 60-day inverse-volatility weighting generates persistent risk-adjusted excess returns across a decorrelated futures panel spanning Equities (`ES`, `YM`, `RTY`), Energy (`CL`), and Precious Metals (`GC`), outperforming a static multi-asset long-only risk parity benchmark after accounting for empirical central limit order book (CLOB) execution tolls and basis dynamics.

Unlike single-asset equity timing models (e.g. `WEINSTEIN_STAGE_01`) which suffer from sitting in cash during equity bull markets, a multi-asset futures momentum framework:
1. Operates symmetrically **Long and Short** with zero stock-borrow drag.
2. Exploits cross-asset decorrelation (e.g., commodities trending during equity bear markets).
3. Executes on CME CLOB venues where measured execution tolls on micro contracts are 1.0–2.8 bp rather than retail MT5 dealer markups (4.3x / 5.0 bp).

## 2. Frozen Specification — ONE Variant, Zero Sweeps

```
timeframe            Daily (es_daily, ym_daily, rty_daily, gc_daily, cl_daily)
LOOKBACK             252 trading sessions (12 months)
VOL_LOOKBACK         60 trading sessions (trailing standard deviation of daily returns)
VOL_TARGET           10.0% annualized aggregate portfolio volatility
REBALANCE_CADENCE    22 trading sessions (monthly rebalance)
ASSETS               ES, YM, CL, GC (all dates); RTY added post 2017-07-10
WEIGHTING FORMULA    w_i[t] = (1 / N_t) * (VOL_TARGET / sigma_i[t]) * sign(P_i[t] - P_i[t - 252])
                     where sigma_i[t] = std(daily_ret[t-59..t]) * sqrt(252)
                     normalized such that sum(|w_i[t]| * sigma_i[t]) == VOL_TARGET
EXECUTION            Signals evaluated at close[t]; rebalance executes at open[t + 1]
TOLL VENUE           CME CLOB micro contract specs via core/cost_model.py:
                     MES: 1.018 bp, MYM: 0.906 bp, M2K: 1.665 bp, MCL: 2.789 bp, MGC: 1.089 bp
TURNOVER COST        0.5 * toll_i * |w_i[t+1] - w_i[t]| paid on each rebalance
```

**Zero parameter sweeps.** The 252-day momentum lookback, 60-day volatility estimator, 22-day rebalance interval, and 10% portfolio volatility target are frozen directly from Moskowitz et al. (2012). No alternative lookbacks (e.g. 63 days, 126 days) or weighting schemes will be tested.

## 3. Primary Controls

### Control A — Static Risk Parity Long-Only
Identical inverse-volatility sizing and 22-day rebalance calendar, but with sign fixed permanently to `+1.0`:
$$w_{i, t}^{\text{CtrlA}} = \frac{1}{N_t} \cdot \frac{\sigma_{\text{target}}}{\sigma_{i, t}} \times (+1.0)$$
This isolates whether dynamic trend-following alpha exceeds the static risk premia of holding a diversified multi-asset basket of equities and commodities.

### Control B — Random Sign Null (1,000 Monte Carlo Draws)
1,000 synthetic portfolios generated by assigning random signs $\{-1, +1\}$ uniformly at each rebalance date per active asset, preserving exact asset volatility scaling, rebalance dates, and CLOB turnover tolls.
The empirical null distribution $\mathcal{N}(\mu_{\text{ctrlB}}, \sigma_{\text{ctrlB}})$ is estimated directly from this population.

## 4. Pre-Registered Gates

* **Gate 1 — Economic Alpha**:
  Net annualized Sharpe Ratio $\ge 0.60$ and net annualized return $> 0$ after deducting measured CLOB execution tolls on micro contracts.
* **Gate 2 — Trend Alpha over Static Long-Only**:
  Net Sharpe Ratio of the TSMOM strategy must exceed Control A (Static Risk Parity) by at least $+0.20$:
  $$\text{Sharpe}_{\text{TSMOM}} - \text{Sharpe}_{\text{CtrlA}} \ge +0.20$$
* **Gate 3 — Significance Against Random Null**:
  Net Sharpe Ratio must exceed the empirical Control B mean by at least $2.0$ standard errors:
  $$\frac{\text{Sharpe}_{\text{TSMOM}} - \mu_{\text{CtrlB}}}{\sigma_{\text{CtrlB}}} \ge 2.00$$
* **Gate 4 — Temporal Stability Across 4 Chronological Quarters**:
  Net Sharpe Ratio must be strictly positive ($\text{Sharpe} > 0$) in at least 3 of the 4 chronological sub-windows:
  - Window 1: 2011-06-07 to 2014-07-31 (Post-GFC Recovery / Sovereign Debt Crisis)
  - Window 2: 2014-08-01 to 2017-09-30 (Commodity Collapse / China Shock)
  - Window 3: 2017-10-01 to 2020-11-30 (QT / COVID Crash & Rebound)
  - Window 4: 2020-12-01 to 2023-12-29 (Inflation Spike / Global Rate Tightening)

## 6. Honest Prior

Time series momentum on futures is one of the most thoroughly documented empirical asset pricing anomalies in quantitative finance. However, post-2010 performance across trend-following CTAs has experienced notable dispersion and "trend decay" during the ZIRP era (whipsaws in non-trending equity environments).
Because our discovery panel spans 2010 to 2023 (post-GFC through ZIRP and the 2022 rate shock), this test provides an un-mined, causal evaluation of whether 12-month TSMOM retains an economic edge on liquid CME futures after CLOB friction.
